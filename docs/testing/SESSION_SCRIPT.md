# COMMUNIQ Usability Testing Session Script

**Target Duration:** 25 to 35 minutes  
**Roles:** Facilitator, Observer / Note-taker, Participant (AAC User), Communication Partner (Parent / Caregiver / Teacher)

---

## Part 1: Welcome and Warm-Up (5 minutes)

1. **Greeting:**
   - Welcome the participant and their communication partner.
   - Settle in a comfortable, quiet seating arrangement where the screen is easily reachable (tablet or laptop placed on a steady stand or flat surface).
2. **Review Consent:**
   - Explain the consent form in simple terms.
   - Confirm that the participant knows they are in control and can take a break at any moment.
3. **Setup Device:**
   - Open COMMUNIQ in full-screen or standalone PWA window.
   - Confirm the selected mode matches the participant's age:
     - Class 1-7: Child Mode
     - Class 8-12: Student Mode
     - 18+: Adult Mode
   - Confirm the selected language (English, Kannada, or Hindi).
   - In **My Day**, tap **Test Session** to start the evaluation timer.

---

## Part 2: Task 1: Expressing Immediate Needs (5 minutes)

**Goal:** Evaluate navigation speed and card recognition for urgent and daily needs.

1. **Prompt to Participant:**
   - "Imagine you feel thirsty. Show me how you would tell someone you need water."
2. **Observer Checks:**
   - Did the participant find the card directly or use categories?
   - Was the touch target easy to press?
   - Did the voice output speak clearly?
3. **Second Prompt:**
   - "Now imagine you are in pain or need a doctor. Show me how you ask for help."
   - Observer checks if the emergency confirmation modal is understood.

---

## Part 3: Task 2: Two-Way Conversation (8 minutes)

**Goal:** Evaluate partner speech recognition and AI suggestions under "You could say".

1. **Open the Talk screen.**
2. **Turn 1 (Partner Speaks):**
   - Speaking partner taps the microphone and asks: "What would you like to have for lunch today?"
   - Verify speech is transcribed into large readable text.
   - Observe if suggested options appear under "You could say".
   - Participant selects one option. Verify immediate speech output.
3. **Turn 2 (Partner Follow-up):**
   - Speaking partner asks: "Would you like some water or tea with that?"
   - Participant selects or types an answer.
4. **Offline Test:**
   - Disconnect Wi-Fi or turn off AI in Settings.
   - Repeat one conversational turn to verify that local deterministic rules provide immediate suggestions without internet.

---

## Part 4: Task 3: Draw What You Mean (5 minutes)

**Goal:** Evaluate sketchpad usability and symbol matching.

1. **Open Communicate &gt; Something else &gt; Draw.**
2. **Prompt:** "Try sketching an apple, a house, or a car on the pad."
3. **Observe:**
   - Ease of pencil / eraser selection.
   - Participant taps "What did I draw?".
   - Verify recognition result card ("It looks like an Apple. Is this what you mean?").
   - Participant confirms and hears the word spoken aloud.

---

## Part 5: Task 4: Practice Scenario (5 minutes)

**Goal:** Evaluate guided roleplay games and positive reinforcement.

1. **Open Practice screen.**
2. **Select Scenario:** "At a Restaurant" or "In the Classroom".
3. **Run 3 to 4 turns:**
   - Participant reads partner prompt and selects desired response.
   - Observe star milestone rewards and mascot celebrations (in Child mode).

---

## Part 6: Wrap-Up and Debrief (5 minutes)

1. **Stop Test Session:**
   - In **My Day**, tap **End Test**. Record duration, utterances, and words per minute.
2. **Participant Feedback:**
   - Ask the participant to point to how they felt using COMMUNIQ (Happy, Okay, Frustrated).
   - Ask what was easiest and what was hardest.
3. **Caregiver / Therapist Feedback:**
   - Administer the Feedback Form.
   - Thank the participant warmly.
