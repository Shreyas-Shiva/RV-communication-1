# COMMUNIQ Participant and Guardian Consent Form

**Project:** COMMUNIQ (Augmentative and Alternative Communication)  
**Purpose:** Usability evaluation of communication cards, two-way speech assist, sketch recognition, and offline assistive tools.

---

## 1. What This Study Is About

COMMUNIQ is an assistive web application designed for individuals who cannot speak, or who find speaking difficult, to communicate with people who can. We are evaluating how easily participants can express daily needs, participate in conversations, and navigate between categories.

## 2. Voluntary Participation

Your participation (or your child/ward's participation) is completely voluntary:
- You may pause or stop the session at any time for any reason.
- You do not need to answer any questions or try any feature that causes discomfort or fatigue.
- Stopping the session carries no negative consequences whatsoever.

## 3. Data Collection and Privacy Guarantees

We take communication privacy seriously:
- **Local Storage Only:** By default, all conversation logs and card selections stay strictly inside the browser on the test device.
- **No Cloud Voice Storage:** Microphones and camera inputs are used in real-time only to transcribe partner speech and identify gestures. Raw audio recordings, camera videos, and sketch images are never uploaded to remote servers or saved to disk.
- **Anonymity:** All session observation notes and feedback are indexed by participant codes (e.g., P01, P02). No full names or identifying personal records are published.
- **Deletion:** You may ask us to delete all session logs from the testing device immediately at the end of the session.
