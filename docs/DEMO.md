# COMMUNIQ Two-Minute Live Demonstration Script

**Tagline:** "Everyone deserves a voice."  
**Supporting Line:** "Communicate naturally. Connect confidently."  
**Target Duration:** Exactly 2 minutes (120 seconds).

---

## [0:00 - 0:25] Introduction & The Core Philosophy

> **Speaker (Facing Audience / Camera):**  
> "Imagine having thoughts, warmth, needs, and humor, but being unable to speak them aloud.  
> This is COMMUNIQ. An augmentative and alternative communication app built for people who cannot speak, or find speaking very hard, to connect naturally with people who can.  
> 
> Our most important rule: the human communicator is always at the center. Communication never depends on internet or AI. Everything you see works 100% offline."

---

## [0:25 - 0:45] Instant Communication (Picture Cards)

> **Speaker (Showing the Screen on Communicate tab):**  
> "Let us start with daily needs. A child or elder opens the Communicate screen.  
> They tap 'Food & Drink', and tap 'Water'."  
> *(Speaker taps Water card)*  
> **COMMUNIQ Voice Speaks:** *"Water."*  
> 
> "It speaks instantly through the device speaker. No delay, no cloud latency.  
> In Student or Adult mode, they can tap 'I want' followed by 'Warm rice', and the app speaks a full natural sentence: *'I want warm rice please.'* Every spoken word is saved safely on this device in My Day."

---

## [0:45 - 1:15] Two-Way Conversation Loop (Talk Screen)

> **Speaker (Navigating to Talk screen):**  
> "Now let us see what happens when someone talks to them.  
> I am the speaking partner. I tap the microphone and ask:  
> *'Welcome! What would you like for lunch today?'*"  
> *(Partner speech appears in large readable letters on screen)*  
> 
> "COMMUNIQ listens and instantly provides 3 to 6 context-aware options under 'You could say'.  
> The non-speaking user looks at the options and taps: *'I would like pizza please.'*"  
> *(User taps the option)*  
> **COMMUNIQ Voice Speaks:** *"I would like pizza please."*  
> 
> "Hear, Understand, Suggest, Tap, Speak, and Predict Next. The loop continues naturally without pressure."

---

## [1:15 - 1:40] Draw What You Mean & Practice

> **Speaker (Opening Communicate > Something else > Draw):**  
> "What if the user wants something not on their board?  
> They can draw what they mean. Watch: I sketch a simple circle with a stem."  
> *(Speaker draws quick sketch and taps 'What did I draw?')*  
> **COMMUNIQ Displays:** *"It looks like an Apple. Is this what you mean?"*  
> *(User confirms, audio speaks: "Apple" and adds to the sentence bar)*  
> 
> "And in our **Practice** screen, communicators can roleplay 8 everyday scenarios like ordering at a restaurant or talking at school, earning cheerful stars and communication streaks."

---

## [1:40 - 2:00] Multilingual, Private & Offline Guarantee

> **Speaker (Switching language to Kannada or Hindi in Settings):**  
> "COMMUNIQ supports English, Kannada, and Hindi in native scripts.  
> There is zero purple, zero gradients, zero emoji icons, and zero telemetry tracking scripts.  
> 
> Even if I turn off Wi-Fi right now, COMMUNIQ continues working seamlessly.  
> Because everyone deserves a voice."
